## Overview

A forecasting prototype that predicts final productivity outcomes from evolving schedule signals such as ramp deviation, peak labor shifts, baseline execution, and critical-path volatility.

## Goals

- Predict final productivity outcomes while a project is still in flight.
- Translate evolving schedule signals into a forward-looking estimate.
- Give teams time to intervene before outcomes are locked in.

## Approach

- Derive schedule signals: ramp deviation, peak labor shifts, baseline execution, critical-path volatility.
- Train a forecasting model on evolving, time-indexed project data.
- Evaluate forecast accuracy at successive project stages.
