## Overview

A synthetic construction analytics project that forecasts project risk using leading indicators, historical trends, and threshold-based classification.

## Goals

- Surface emerging project risk early from leading indicators rather than lagging outcomes.
- Combine historical trends with threshold-based rules into a single, interpretable signal.
- Provide a defensible, explainable basis for prioritizing attention across projects.

## Approach

- Engineer leading-indicator features from synthetic project telemetry.
- Train and calibrate a classification model against threshold-defined risk bands.
- Validate against historical trends and stress-test the decision thresholds.
