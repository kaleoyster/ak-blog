## Overview

A job-level scoring framework that combines inspection coverage, high-risk observations, workforce spread, and critical activity controls into a single risk score.

## Goals

- Roll up disparate job-site signals into one comparable risk score.
- Weight inspection coverage, observations, and controls transparently.
- Make the score actionable inside a day-to-day operations dashboard.

## Approach

- Define and normalize component signals across jobs.
- Compose a weighted scoring model with tunable, documented weights.
- Surface the score and its drivers in an interactive dashboard.
