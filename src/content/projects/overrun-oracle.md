## Overview

A risk-banding tool that estimates potential labor overrun exposure using predicted productivity, peer-group benchmarks, and percentile-based loss ranges.

## Goals

- Quantify potential labor overrun exposure, not just a point estimate.
- Anchor estimates against peer-group benchmarks for context.
- Express exposure as interpretable percentile-based loss ranges.

## Approach

- Feed predicted productivity into a simulation of overrun outcomes.
- Benchmark each project against comparable peer groups.
- Band the results into percentile ranges for decision-making.
