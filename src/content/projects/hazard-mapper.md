## Overview

An NLP prototype that maps safety observations into standardized risk categories to support trend analysis and preventative action.

## Goals

- Standardize free-text safety observations into consistent risk categories.
- Enable trend analysis across large volumes of observations.
- Support earlier, preventative action.

## Approach

- Build an NLP/LLM pipeline to classify observations into a taxonomy.
- Evaluate categorization quality against human-labeled samples.
- Aggregate categorized data for trend reporting.
