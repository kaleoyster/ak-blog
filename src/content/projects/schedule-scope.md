## Overview

An interactive analysis of how schedule instability, scope growth, critical-path changes, and labor ramp shifts relate to project outcomes.

## Goals

- Relate schedule instability and scope growth to project outcomes.
- Make the relationships explorable rather than buried in a report.
- Identify which schedule dynamics matter most.

## Approach

- Engineer schedule-dynamics features: instability, scope growth, critical-path changes, ramp shifts.
- Fit regression models linking those features to outcomes.
- Present findings through interactive visualizations.
