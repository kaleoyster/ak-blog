## Overview

A benchmarking project that studies how staff ratios, project duration, subcontractor mix, and market segment affect indirect cost reliability.

## Goals

- Understand what drives indirect cost reliability across projects.
- Quantify the effect of staffing ratios, duration, and subcontractor mix.
- Establish defensible benchmarks by market segment.

## Approach

- Assemble a benchmarking dataset across projects and segments.
- Model indirect cost reliability against the candidate drivers.
- Report effect sizes and segment-level benchmarks.
